Research

My research program centers on one question: how can AI tell whether information is still appropriate to use now? My dissertation develops a framework for characterizing temporal change in knowledge-grounded AI, supported by work in explainable AI, medical vision-language systems, and video understanding.

Dissertation Research

Characterizing Temporal Change for Reliable Knowledge-Grounded AI. My dissertation asks how AI systems can tell whether retrieved information is still appropriate to use now — not merely whether it is relevant or recent. Timeliness is treated as distinct from recency: older information may remain useful, and the newest information is not always the most appropriate.

Research Directions

  • Faithful XAI for Medical Image Report Generation (in progress). A unified evaluation framework that links visual saliency maps, model reasoning chains, and clinical correctness metrics to test whether explanations from chest X-ray report-generation models are faithful to the model's actual reasoning — not just plausible-sounding. The goal is to reduce confident-but-unfaithful explanations in clinical AI.
  • Soccer Action Detection Using TimeSformer. Transformer-based video understanding for context-dependent soccer actions (e.g., pass, shot, dribble) on the SoccerAct10 dataset, reaching 91.13% accuracy with divided space-time attention. Published in the Journal of Computing Sciences in Colleges (ACM Digital Library), 2026. [ACM]
  • Explainable Image Forgery Detection. A ResNet50 + U-Net + Grad-CAM pipeline that both detects manipulated images and localizes the forged regions — 80.32% accuracy and 0.88 forged recall on CASIA 2.0. A companion multi-branch EfficientNet-B3 model adds DCT frequency-domain features.
  • NewsLogic: AI-Powered Fake News Detection. An end-to-end fake-news classifier combining a BERT-tiny + DistilBERT ensemble with LLaMA-4-Scout generated explanations — 83.1% accuracy and 0.91 AUC across 71 live-app cases, deployed as a FastAPI service.
  • Fish Disease Detection with FishNet CNN. CNN-based 7-class disease detection for South Asian freshwater fish aquaculture, reaching ~92.5% test accuracy with a transfer-learning baseline comparison. Presented at the ATU Scholars Symposium, 2024.
  • MLBioIGE: Machine Learning and Bioinformatics for Pulmonary Fibrosis. Integrated machine learning with DESeq2-based bioinformatics analysis to identify genetic effects of SARS-CoV-2 on idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis patients, with visualizations of differentially expressed genes. Published in Biology Methods and Protocols, 2022.
  • Programming Sequence Improvement Program (PSIP). A multi-year study of retention and learning outcomes across the undergraduate computer science programming sequence, with aggregated grade data and survey instruments. Published at the IEEE HCIRA International Congress, 2023.
