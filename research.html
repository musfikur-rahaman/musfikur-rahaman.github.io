Research

Research Themes & Technical Direction

My research focuses on Explainable AI (XAI), multimodal AI systems, computer vision, and transformer-based deep learning. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that are not only accurate, but also reliable, interpretable, and suitable for high-stakes or complex real-world settings.

Theme 1: Explainable & Trustworthy AI

I study how AI systems can provide explanations that are faithful to model behavior rather than simply plausible to users.

  • Explanation faithfulness: evaluating whether explanations reflect the evidence actually used by the model.
  • Hallucination detection: identifying unsupported or clinically inaccurate content in model-generated outputs.
  • Visual interpretability: saliency-based approaches such as Grad-CAM for understanding model attention.
  • Reliability: error analysis, confidence-aware evaluation, and robustness under difficult cases.

Theme 2: Multimodal & Medical AI

My ongoing work examines trustworthy multimodal AI through medical image report generation, where systems must align visual evidence with generated clinical language.

  • Medical vision-language models for chest X-ray report generation.
  • Connecting visual saliency, model reasoning, and clinical correctness.
  • Evaluating hallucinations and explanation faithfulness in generated reports.
  • Developing unified evaluation frameworks for trustworthy medical AI.

Theme 3: Transformers for Video Understanding

I use transformer architectures to model long-range spatiotemporal relationships in video and improve fine-grained action recognition.

  • TimeSformer: transformer-based soccer action recognition using divided space-time attention.
  • Temporal context: studying ambiguous actions that depend on what occurs before and after an event.
  • Sports analytics: automated recognition of fine-grained actions in broadcast soccer video.
  • Generalization: evaluating robustness across visually similar classes and changing broadcast conditions.

Theme 4: Applied AI, Forensics & Information Quality

I also develop applied AI systems in image forensics, misinformation detection, and biomedical analysis.

  • Image manipulation detection and localization using ResNet50, U-Net, and Grad-CAM.
  • Transformer-based fake news classification with transparent explanation generation.
  • Machine learning and bioinformatics for biomedical insight discovery.
  • Responsible deployment through interpretable outputs and traceable system design.
