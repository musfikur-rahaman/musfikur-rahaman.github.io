Research

Research & Publications

My research focuses on computer vision, video understanding, Explainable AI, and multimodal systems, with major work in soccer action detection and faithful AI for medical image report generation. I am especially interested in models that are accurate, context-aware, interpretable, and reliable in real-world settings.

Soccer Action Detection & Sports Video Understanding

Computer Vision • Sports Analytics • Transformers

Transformer-Based Soccer Action Recognition

My soccer-video research began with League of Learning: Deep Learning for Soccer Action Video Classification using ConvLSTM and LRCN models and later progressed to transformer-based video understanding.

In my 2026 publication, Soccer Action Detection Using TimeSformer: A Transformer-Based Approach to Video Understanding, the TimeSformer model achieved 91.13% validation accuracy on SoccerAct10. The results also highlighted challenges in distinguishing context-dependent actions such as fouls, short passes, and long passes.

  • Fine-grained soccer action recognition and temporal reasoning.
  • Sports-video event understanding, indexing, and performance analysis.
  • Broader applications in human activity recognition and complex video understanding.

Explainable & Multimodal AI

Ongoing • Medical AI • XAI • Multimodal Systems

Beyond the Output: Faithful XAI for Medical Image Report Generation

This work investigates hallucination detection and explanation faithfulness in medical vision-language models using chest X-ray report generation as a testbed.

  • Evaluate whether generated clinical findings are grounded in visual evidence.
  • Identify hallucinated or unsupported statements in generated reports.
  • Connect visual saliency, model reasoning, and clinical correctness in a unified evaluation framework.

Future Research Directions

Longer Temporal Context

Study longer video sequences and sequence-aware models so actions can be interpreted using what occurs before, during, and after an event.

Multimodal Context

Combine video with player movement, pose, field position, audio, commentary, and other contextual information to improve recognition of ambiguous actions.

Robustness & Generalization

Evaluate models across different camera angles, lighting, stadium settings, zoom levels, graphics, and broadcast production styles.

Explainability & Efficiency

Develop interpretable and more efficient video models that support error analysis, trustworthy predictions, and faster or near-real-time analysis.
