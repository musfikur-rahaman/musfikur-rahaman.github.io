Academic Portfolio

Musfikur Rahaman

I am a PhD student in Information Science at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, where I continue to develop my academic work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, information technology, and applied computational research. My academic preparation includes graduate and undergraduate training in information technology, supported by experience in research, graduate assistantships, teaching support, student mentoring, and interdisciplinary academic collaboration.

A major direction of my research is soccer action detection and sports video understanding. I study how deep learning and transformer-based video models can recognize complex actions that unfold over time, especially when visually similar events depend on temporal context. This work connects broader problems in computer vision, human activity recognition, temporal reasoning, multimodal learning, model reliability, and explainability.

My scholarly background also includes peer-reviewed publications in biomedical informatics and computing education, alongside research in explainable and trustworthy AI, medical vision-language systems, image forensics, and information credibility. Across these activities, I have contributed to research design, machine learning model development, data analysis, experimental evaluation, scientific writing, and academic presentation.

In addition to research, I have supported university instruction and academic operations through graduate assistant roles at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock and Arkansas Tech University, as well as earlier research experience at the University of Information Technology & Sciences in Bangladesh. These experiences have helped me build a broad academic profile combining research, teaching, technical development, and service.

Academic Interests

My academic interests focus on developing intelligent systems that can understand complex visual and multimodal information while remaining accurate, explainable, and trustworthy.

  • Soccer Action Detection & Sports Video Understanding — fine-grained action recognition, event understanding, sports analytics, and automated interpretation of complex soccer video.
  • Transformer-Based Video Understanding & Temporal Reasoning — TimeSformer, long-range temporal context, sequence-aware modeling, and recognition of actions that depend on what happens before and after an event.
  • Computer Vision & Human Activity Recognition — deep learning methods for visual recognition, spatiotemporal representation learning, and complex real-world activity understanding.
  • Multimodal AI & Vision-Language Models — combining video, images, language, audio, and contextual information for richer and more reliable AI understanding.
  • Explainable & Trustworthy AI — model interpretability, explanation faithfulness, visual grounding, hallucination detection, reliability, and transparent AI evaluation.
  • Faithful XAI for Medical Image Report Generation — research related to Beyond the Output: Faithful XAI for Medical Image Report Generation, including medical vision-language models, chest X-ray report generation, grounded explanations, and clinically reliable AI outputs.
  • Generative AI & Responsible AI — reliable generation, multimodal reasoning, information quality, and responsible deployment of AI systems.
  • Applied AI in Healthcare, Bioinformatics & Information Systems — machine learning applications across medical imaging, biomedical data, information credibility, and interdisciplinary computational research.
