About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Information Science at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock. My dissertation asks a foundational question for knowledge-grounded AI: how can we tell whether retrieved information is still appropriate to use now? Timeliness is not simply recency — older information can remain useful, and newer information is not always the most appropriate.

My dissertation develops a framework for characterizing temporal change in knowledge-grounded AI. Beyond that, my work spans explainable and trustworthy AI, medical vision-language systems, computer vision, and multimodal learning, with peer-reviewed publications in the ACM Digital Library, IEEE proceedings, and Biology Methods and Protocols.

Projects

All projects are openly maintained on GitHub.

  • Beyond the Output: Faithful XAI for Medical Image Report Generation [code]
    A unified evaluation framework linking visual saliency maps, model reasoning chains, and clinical correctness to reduce unfaithful explanations in chest X-ray report generation. In progress.
  • Soccer Action Detection Using TimeSformer [code]
    Transformer-based video understanding for context-dependent soccer actions (91.13% on SoccerAct10). Full analysis notebook, project report, and published JCSC/ACM paper.
  • NewsLogic: AI-Powered Fake News Detection [code]
    End-to-end fake news classifier — BERT-tiny + DistilBERT ensemble with LLaMA-4-Scout explanations — 83.1% accuracy and 0.91 AUC on 71 live-app cases, deployed as a FastAPI service.
  • Fish Disease Detection with FishNet CNN [code]
    CNN-based disease detection for South Asian freshwater fish aquaculture — 7-class classification at ~92.5% test accuracy.
  • MLBioIGE: ML + Bioinformatics for Pulmonary Fibrosis [code]
    Peer-reviewed study on genetic factors in pulmonary fibrosis among COVID-19 patients, with DESeq2 notebooks and visualizations. Biology Methods and Protocols, 2022.
  • Explainable Image Forgery Detection [code]
    ResNet50 + U-Net + Grad-CAM pipeline for detecting and localizing image manipulation — 80.32% accuracy, 0.88 forged recall on CASIA 2.0.
  • Programming Sequence Improvement Program (PSIP) [code]
    Multi-year study on retention and learning outcomes in undergraduate CS programming courses. IEEE HCIRA 2023 paper, poster, datasets, and survey instruments.
