Research Snapshot

My work investigates how advanced AI systems can achieve strong predictive performance while remaining interpretable, reliable, and clinically or practically meaningful. I am particularly interested in explanation faithfulness, hallucination detection, transformer-based video understanding, and transparent AI-assisted decision support.

Focus Areas

  • Explainable & Trustworthy AI: explanation faithfulness, hallucination detection, Grad-CAM, and model behavior analysis.
  • Multimodal & Transformer Models: vision-language models, ViT, TimeSformer, and BERT-family architectures.
  • Healthcare & Bioinformatics: medical image report generation and machine-learning-assisted biomedical analysis.
  • Computer Vision & Information Quality: video understanding, image forensics, and credibility assessment.

Core Technical Stack

  • AI/ML: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, HuggingFace Transformers, scikit-learn
  • Models: ViT, TimeSformer, BERT, DistilBERT, LLaMA, ResNet50, U-Net
  • Programming: Python, SQL, C++, Java
  • Systems: FastAPI, REST APIs, Git/GitHub, Supabase, Render
  • Data: MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SQL Server
