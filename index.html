Musfikur Rahaman

Studying when AI’s knowledge stops being trustworthy.

Language models learn facts once, but the world keeps changing. My dissertation looks inside frozen language models to see how they store the old and new versions of a changed fact, comparing Transformers with Mamba state-space models using topological data analysis. My broader work spans explainable AI, medical vision-language systems, and computer vision.

Research focus

How language models represent facts that change over time, building on earlier work in explainability and vision.

All research

Temporal change in language models

My dissertation: how frozen models internally represent superseded and latest versions of the same fact, and whether Transformers and Mamba state-space models store them differently.

Topology of model representations

Using persistent homology to measure the shape of hidden-state point clouds, and testing whether it reveals structure that linear probes, CKA, and MMD miss.

Explainable AI & vision

Earlier work on faithful explanations for medical report generation, auditable image forgery detection, and video understanding with transformers.
