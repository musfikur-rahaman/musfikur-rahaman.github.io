Dissertation Research

The core of my PhD work — an empirically validated framework for measuring temporal appropriateness in knowledge-grounded AI.

Dissertation · In Progress

Characterizing Temporal Change for Reliable Knowledge-Grounded AI

My dissertation asks whether the information AI retrieves is still appropriate to use now — not just whether it is relevant. It develops an empirically validated framework for characterizing temporal change in knowledge-grounded AI systems.
